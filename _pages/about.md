## About Me

I am Yubing Li, a fourth-year Ph.D. student at the School of Information and Intelligent Science in the Donghua University. I am currently conducting visiting research at Nanyang Technological University (NTU) and the Institute for Infocomm Research (I2R) in the A*STAR, under the supervision of Principal Scientist [Dr. Xulei Yang](https://dawdleryang.github.io/) and Senior Scientist [Dr. Xun Xu](https://alex-xun-xu.github.io/).

## Research

My research interests include **Multimodal and Multidimensional Information Processing** (**Text, Images, and Point Clouds**), **Visual Reasoning**, **Brain-Inspired Intelligence** and **Cognitive Science**.

My long-term vision is to advance AI beyond pattern recognition toward a genuine understanding of the world, enabling systems to reason about real-world situations and uncover the underlying principles governing natural phenomena.
